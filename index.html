What We Do

We prepare defense-sector companies to walk into any investor meeting with confidence and connect them with the right people once they're ready.

📊

Pitch Deck Review

We conduct a thorough review of your pitch deck, evaluating structure, clarity, and overall investor readiness.

📝

Narrative Advisement

We refine your company's story so it communicates mission, capability, and market opportunity clearly and compellingly.

💰

Budget Analysis

We assess your financial projections and budget assumptions to ensure they hold up under investor scrutiny.

⚠️

Risk Framing

We help you identify, address, and appropriately frame operational, regulatory, and market risks for investor audiences.

🎤

Investor Q&A Preparation

We rigorously question your team in mock-investor scenarios to ensure you're ready to handle tough questions with confidence.

🤝

Investor Introductions

Once your materials and team are investor-ready, we facilitate introductions to potential investors within our network.
